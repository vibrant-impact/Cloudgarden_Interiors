Our Story

CloudGarden Interiors was founded in 2020 with a singular focus: to reconnect modern living with the natural world. We are an interior design firm specializing in biophilic design and natural aesthetics, creating environments that promote health, productivity, and peace.

Our Core Principles

Wellness

Designing spaces that contribute directly to mental and physical health.

Creativity

Pushing the boundaries of natural aesthetics to deliver unique and inspiring environments.

Sustainability

Prioritizing eco-friendly materials and practices in every project.

The Team

We are a dedicated team of 25 employees, including designers, horticulturalists, and project managers.

Our diverse expertise ensures every project—from small consultations to full commercial installations—is executed with precision and care.
